How Cridaa works

1 · Share your area

Allow location access or type any neighbourhood to see box cricket turfs, football grounds and badminton courts around you.

2 · Compare venues

Filter by sport, sort by distance or rating, and check real photos and Google reviews before you decide.

3 · Get in touch

Tap “Call Now”, tell us a little about yourself and get the venue's number. Transparent pricing is coming soon.

FAQ

How do I find a box cricket turf near me?

Allow location access on Cridaa and choose the “Box Cricket” filter. Venues are listed nearest first, with ratings and photos.

Can I browse turfs in another city?

Yes. Use the search bar or city dropdown at the top to switch to any neighbourhood or city.

When will turf prices be listed?

Pricing and instant booking are on our roadmap – every card carries a “Prices Coming Soon” badge until then.

Where does venue data come from?

Names, ratings and photos come from Google Maps Platform. We never store your location beyond your browser session.
